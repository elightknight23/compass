# Examination Bylaws

These bylaws govern the conduct of internal tests and end-semester
examinations. They are administered by the Controller of Examinations.

## Hall Tickets

A hall ticket is issued only to students who are eligible under the
Academic Regulations. A student without a hall ticket will not be admitted
to the examination hall. Hall tickets are released one week before the
first examination.

## Conduct in the Examination Hall

Students must be seated ten minutes before the start of the examination. No
student is admitted more than thirty minutes after the start, and no student
may leave in the first hour.

Mobile phones, smart watches and any written material are prohibited inside
the hall, whether switched on or not.

## Malpractice

Possession of prohibited material, communication with another candidate, or
impersonation is malpractice. The invigilator will take the answer script
and report the matter. The Malpractice Committee may cancel the examination
concerned, cancel all examinations of that semester, or debar the student
from one or more subsequent examination sessions.

## Absence from an Examination

A student who is absent from an end-semester examination is graded as absent
for that course and must re-appear when it is next conducted. Absence for a
medical reason does not change the grade, but a student hospitalised during
the examinations may apply to the Controller for permission to write the
supplementary examination, with a certificate, within 5 working days.

Absence from an internal test for a medical reason may be made up by a
re-test, at the course teacher's discretion, if applied for within 3 working
days.

## Revaluation

A student may apply for revaluation of an end-semester answer script within
7 days of the publication of results, on payment of the revaluation fee. The
revalued mark replaces the original mark only if it is higher.

## Results

Results are published on the College portal. A student with outstanding dues
will have their result withheld until the dues are cleared.
