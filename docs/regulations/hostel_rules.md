# Hostel Rules

These rules apply to every resident of the College hostels. They are
administered by the hostel wardens.

## Admission and Rooms

Hostel rooms are allotted for one academic year at a time. Residents may
not change rooms without the warden's permission, and may not sublet or
share a room with a non-resident.

Residents are responsible for the furniture and fittings in their room. Loss
or damage beyond normal wear is charged to the resident.

## In-Time and Movement Register

The hostel gates close at 9:00 PM. A resident returning later must have the
warden's prior permission.

A resident leaving the hostel overnight must make an entry in the movement
register, with their destination and expected return. A movement register
entry is not academic leave: absence from classes still needs leave from the
faculty advisor under the OD and Leave Policy.

## Visitors

Visitors are permitted in the visitors' lounge between 4:00 PM and 7:00 PM.
Visitors are not permitted in residents' rooms.

## Mess

Meals are served in the hostel mess at the times displayed on the mess notice
board. Mess charges are payable each semester in advance. A resident who will
be away for five or more consecutive days may apply to the warden for a mess
rebate, at least two days before leaving.

## Electrical Appliances

Electric heaters, induction stoves and irons are not permitted in rooms.
Laptops, phone chargers and table lamps are permitted.

## Maintenance and Complaints

Faults in rooms and common areas — fans, lights, plumbing, water supply,
furniture — are reported as facility complaints with the hostel category.
They are resolved by the hostel office, and the warden marks them resolved
once the work is done. See the Campus Facilities policy for response times.

## Discipline

Ragging is a criminal offence and is dealt with under the College's
anti-ragging regulations. Possession or consumption of alcohol or drugs on
campus leads to expulsion from the hostel.
