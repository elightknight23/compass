# Campus Facilities

This policy covers the library, the laboratories and College transport, and
explains how facility complaints are handled.

## Library

### Hours

The central library is open from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM on working days and from
9:00 AM to 1:00 PM on Saturdays. It is closed on Sundays and College
holidays. During the end-semester examinations the reading room stays open
until 10:00 PM.

### Borrowing

A student may borrow up to four books at a time for fourteen days. A loan
may be renewed once if nobody else has reserved the book. Reference books and
journals may not be borrowed.

### Fines

A fine of two rupees per day is charged for each book returned late. Lost
books must be replaced or paid for at the current price.

## Laboratories

Students must wear closed shoes in all laboratories and a lab coat in the
chemistry and electronics laboratories. Food and drink are not permitted.

Faulty or damaged equipment must be reported to the lab in-charge
immediately and logged as a facility complaint with the lab category. A
student is not charged for equipment that fails in normal use.

## Transport

### Bus Passes

College buses run on fixed routes. A bus pass is issued for one semester and
must be shown on boarding. A student may travel only on the route printed on
their pass.

### Timings

Buses reach the College by 8:30 AM and leave at 4:30 PM. A bus will not wait
for a student who is not at the stop at the scheduled time.

### Transport Complaints

Missed stops, overcrowding, rash driving and breakdowns are reported as
facility complaints with the transport category.

## How Facility Complaints Are Handled

### Categories

Every facility complaint is filed under one of four categories: hostel, lab,
library or transport. The category decides which office works on it.

### Priority and Response Targets

| Priority | Examples | Response target |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| High | No water or power, a safety hazard | within 24 hours |
| Medium | A broken fan, a faulty lab instrument | within 3 working days |
| Low | Cosmetic damage, a suggestion | within 7 working days |

A complaint filed without a stated priority is treated as medium.

### Resolution

Facility complaints are resolved by the hostel and campus facilities office,
under the hostel warden. A complaint is marked resolved when the work is done.
The student who filed it may withdraw it at any time before then — for
example, if the problem has gone away.
