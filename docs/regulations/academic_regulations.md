# Academic Regulations

These regulations govern the structure, assessment and progression of the
College's four-year undergraduate programmes.

## Programme Structure

Each programme runs for eight semesters over four years. An odd semester
runs from July to November and an even semester from December to April.
Each semester has at least 90 working days of instruction.

## Credits

Every course carries a number of credits, normally three or four for a
theory course and one or two for a laboratory course. A student must earn
the total credits specified for their programme to graduate.

## Assessment

Each theory course is assessed out of 100 marks:

- **Continuous internal assessment — 40 marks**, made up of two internal
  tests, assignments and a seminar or quiz.
- **End-semester examination — 60 marks**, conducted by the Controller of
  Examinations.

A student must score at least 50% of the combined marks, and at least 45%
in the end-semester examination, to pass a course.

## Eligibility for End-Semester Examinations

To be eligible to write the end-semester examinations a student must:

1. meet the attendance requirement in the Attendance Regulations, or have a
   shortage condoned under those regulations; and
2. have no dues outstanding to the College, the library or the hostel.

## Grades and CGPA

Results are published as letter grades on a ten-point scale:

| Grade | Grade point | Marks |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| O | 10 | 91–100 |
| A+ | 9 | 81–90 |
| A | 8 | 71–80 |
| B+ | 7 | 61–70 |
| B | 6 | 56–60 |
| C | 5 | 50–55 |
| RA | 0 | below 50 (re-appearance) |

The Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) is the credit-weighted average of
grade points over all courses passed.

## Arrears

A course graded RA is an arrear. The student may re-appear for the
end-semester examination of that course when it is next conducted, without
re-attending the classes, provided their attendance for that course was not
short.

## Academic Integrity

Copying in assignments, plagiarism in project reports and impersonation are
academic misconduct. They are dealt with under the Examination Bylaws.
